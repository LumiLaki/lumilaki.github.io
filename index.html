07 · CASE STUDY

KINETA · question hook

Spec work for an invented brand. No ad spend or results yet: this shows how I would build and test it.

GoalTest a question hook against a weak, brand first opener.
AudienceRunners and endurance athletes who drink plain water after long workouts.
ConceptPlain water. After a ninety minute workout? The problem on screen first, then the pour, then the offer.
ProcessScript in one copy file, voice by ElevenLabs, product photos by ChatGPT Images, pour clip by Kling, then my Python and FFmpeg render with word timed captions and a contrast check on every caption.
My partScript, art direction, prompts, edit, sound mix and the batch system. The brand is invented.
Deliverables9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and a 6 second cut, a Spanish version and three new hooks rendered in 2.5 minutes.

How the system works

1 · CopyHooks, bodies and offers live in one file. Changing a line changes only the ads that use it.
2 · VoiceEach line is voiced once with ElevenLabs and cached, with word timings that drive the captions.
3 · VisualsOne shared library of product and lifestyle photos, so every ad stays on brand.
4 · RenderA Python and FFmpeg template builds every ad in parallel: cuts on the beat, captions word by word, offer card, in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and a 6 second cut.
5 · CheckEvery ad gets a contact sheet and an automatic contrast check on every caption before it ships.
